J Diddles Seafood & Massage is a fictional parody of spa culture, restaurant marketing, and suspiciously specific places on the internet.

Yes, the whole thing is a joke.

No business operates through this website. There is no buffet, no massage appointment, and no army of tiny fish waiting to work on your back. The experiences, branding, and booking calendar are fictional.

So why can I make an account?

Because a fully functional booking system is an impressively committed way to tell a joke. Your account and saved reservations are real website records. The services they describe are not.

Found us on a map?

A map pin doesn’t make this a real destination. Please don’t travel anywhere expecting service. This website does not verify or endorse any listing and is not affiliated with Google Maps, a real business, or any real person.

No animals. No treatments. No charges.
