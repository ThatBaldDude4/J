Privacy & cookies.

The spa is imaginary. Your privacy matters in the real world.

This notice describes the included app. The person deploying it must add their operator name and privacy contact and review their Railway settings before accepting public registrations.

What we store

If you create an account, we store your nickname, normalized email address, a salted password hash (not your readable password), and when you registered. We store your pretend reservation’s experience, date, time, guest count, status, and creation time. We also store session records and temporary rate-limit counters.

Why we store it

To let you sign in, save and cancel pretend reservations, protect accounts, and prevent slots from being overbooked. We don’t collect card details, phone numbers, health information, or marketing preferences.

Cookies and browser storage

jd_session (local development) or __Host-jd_session (production) is an essential, first-party sign-in and security cookie. It holds a random token, is inaccessible to JavaScript, uses SameSite=Lax, and expires after 7 days. Production uses HTTPS-only Secure cookies. The database stores a hash of the token. Sign-out invalidates the current session.

jd_cookie_notice is a localStorage preference that remembers that you dismissed the cookie explanation. It contains only “dismissed,” stays in this browser until cleared, and is not sent to the server. You can reopen the notice using “Cookie info” in the footer. If browser storage is unavailable, the website still works.

There are no analytics, advertising, optional tracking cookies, embedded maps, third-party fonts, or social tracking widgets in this project. Essential session cookies enable sign-in; you can browse the informational pages without an account. Blocking the session cookie prevents sign-in and bookings.

Hosting and sharing

When the operator deploys this app on Railway, Railway processes app and PostgreSQL data to provide hosting. The app uses your connection IP to generate a hashed, temporary abuse-prevention key; raw IP addresses are not stored by this app. Hosting providers may independently retain request metadata, IP addresses, and operational logs according to their policies. The app does not sell your data or send marketing messages.

Retention and deletion

Account details and reservations remain until you delete your account. Account deletion removes your profile, reservations, and all associated sessions from the live database. Expired sessions and rate-limit records are removed on a later API request, with cleanup attempted hourly. Rate-limit windows last at most one hour; session expiry is 7 days. A host’s backups may retain older records according to the operator’s backup policy.

Your controls

View your profile and delete your account from My account. View or cancel pretend reservations in My reservations. Clear this site’s browser data to remove the notice preference and cookie; this does not delete database records. Contact the site operator for other privacy or access requests.

Operator & contact

The operator has not supplied public contact details yet. This deployment should remain a private demo until they do.

Registration does not verify email ownership. No password-reset email service is included. Use a unique password and do not enter sensitive personal information.
